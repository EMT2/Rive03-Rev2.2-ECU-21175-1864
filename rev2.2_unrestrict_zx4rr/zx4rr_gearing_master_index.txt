[2] Rev2.2 TUNING ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
* Over-Rev Cushion: Opening a 1,000 RPM window past peak power removes 
  the need for clumsy, temporary upshifts right before a corner 
  braking zone, allowing the chassis to stay fully settled.
  
* Mechanical Tolerances: Factory valve springs maintain a safe 500 RPM 
  buffer before hitting valve float limits (>16,000 RPM). Reciprocating 
  mass stress levels remain comfortably within standard metallurgical 
  fatigue boundaries.

* Low/Full Power Map: ETV map capped to ~34% maximum airflow at the bottom 
  end. Grid interpolation errors eliminated to ensure smooth, linear 
  throttle tracking and predictable delivery. Restrictions removed.

* Back-Torque Management: Integrated anti-wheel-hop engine braking 
  control curves mitigate wheel hop and smooth chassis transitions 
  during rapid corner entry downshifts.
